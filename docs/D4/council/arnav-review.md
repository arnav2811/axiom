## 1. D1-to-D4 Continuity

**Finding A1:** The D4 increment correctly implements the D2 selected concept ("Footprint Lens: Contextual Choice Coach") and preserves the five design principles (DP1–DP5). ## 2. User Needs and JTBD Alignment

**Finding A3:** Sprint 1 delivers the core "understand my footprint" journey. However, the single numeric category (ELECTRICITY) limits the user's ability to understand their overall footprint. **Recommendation:** Sprint 2 must add at least one more numeric category (TRAVEL) to demonstrate breadth.

**Finding A4:** The action coaching tab is functional but basic. The D2 design promised "personalised, context-aware coaching." Current coaching messages are category-generic. **Recommendation:** Sprint 2 should add at least 2 coaching rules per category with context-specific advice.

**Finding A6:** The report correctly avoids claiming activities as completed by specific students without genuine evidence. Contribution plan describes work packages without fabricating hours or personal experiences.
