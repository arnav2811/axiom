# Task Instructions — Synthetic Scenario Cards

**Study:** Footprint Lens Usability Evaluation  
**Instructions:** Present one card at a time, in order. Participant reads aloud before starting.  
**Note:** All data is fictional. Participants never enter their own real data.

---

## Card T1: Monthly Electricity Recording

> You live in a shared 3-bedroom student apartment in Bengaluru. Your electricity sub-meter reads **120 kWh** for October. Record this entry as **household electricity** and check what carbon estimate is calculated.

**Completion Criterion:** Activity recorded with correct category (Electricity), quantity (120), and scope (Household). Estimate displayed in table.  
**Comprehension Check:** Participant confirms seeing the calculated estimate and household scope badge.

---

## Card T2: Inspecting Factor Provenance

> You are sceptical of the carbon estimate just displayed. Find out which **emission factor** was used, **where it comes from** (which authority or database), and whether it is **verified or illustrative**.

**Completion Criterion:** Participant navigates to provenance/assumptions view without moderator assistance.  
**Comprehension Check:** Participant correctly identifies factor source (e.g., CEA — Central Electricity Authority), geography (India), and FactorStatus.

---

## Card T3: Protected Choice — Night-Shift Commute

> You work a part-time night shift and must travel **12 km** home by taxi at **2:30 AM** for personal safety (there is no night bus service). Record this journey as a **protected choice**. Check what action recommendations are offered.

**Completion Criterion:** Protected choice flag toggled ON; activity submitted with TRAVEL category.  
**Comprehension Check:** Participant observes that no punitive reduction advice is shown for this protected activity and can explain why.

---

## Card T4: Descriptive Repair Record

> Your kitchen mixer-grinder broke down. Instead of discarding it, you paid a local technician to replace the carbon brushes and **repair it**. Record this repair activity and explain what the system says about emissions.

**Completion Criterion:** Repair/circular category selected; 1 count entered; record created.  
**Comprehension Check:** Participant recognises that the estimate is explicitly "unavailable" with an explanatory reason, not 0 kg CO₂e.

---

## Card T5: Household Scope Review

> Navigate to the **summary view**. What is the total household electricity footprint? Does the system **divide this between roommates** or keep it as a **shared total**? Explain why you think so.

**Completion Criterion:** Participant accesses the summary/dashboard view.  
**Comprehension Check:** Participant states that the household total is kept as a shared aggregate and is not divided per person.
