# Participant Information Sheet

**Study Title:** Usability Evaluation of Footprint Lens Desktop Prototype  
**Investigators:** Arnav Mishra & Keshav Singhal (COMP2300 Group Project)  
**Module:** COMP2300 Software Engineering Group Project  
**Institution:** University of Southampton

---

## What is this study about?

We are evaluating the user interface of **Footprint Lens**, a desktop application prototype for carbon footprint awareness. We want to understand how easy the interface is to use, how clearly it presents information, and whether its features make sense to users.

**We are testing the software, not you.** There are no right or wrong answers, and you cannot do anything wrong during the session.

## What will I be asked to do?

You will be asked to complete **5 short tasks** using pre-prepared synthetic scenario cards. These cards contain fictional data — you will never be asked to enter your own personal information. Each task involves interacting with the Footprint Lens prototype on a laptop.

While completing the tasks, we will ask you to **think aloud** — to tell us what you are looking at, what you expect to happen, and any confusion you experience.

After the tasks, we will ask you to complete a brief usability questionnaire (10 questions, takes about 2 minutes) and answer a few open-ended questions about your experience.

**Total estimated time:** 25–30 minutes.

## What data will be collected?

- **Task completion status** (success/fail for each task)
- **Your verbatim comments** during think-aloud (recorded as written notes, not audio)
- **Questionnaire responses** (anonymous, using a participant code like "P01")

## What data will NOT be collected?

We will **NOT** collect:
- Your name, student ID, or contact details in any observation records
- Real utility bills, rent, financial expenditure, or home address
- Health data, sexual orientation, political opinions, religious beliefs, or any other sensitive personal data as defined by UK GDPR Article 9
- Audio or video recordings

## Is participation voluntary?

**Yes, entirely.** You may:
- Decline to participate without any reason or penalty
- Withdraw at any time during the session without giving a reason
- Skip any task or question you do not wish to answer
- Ask for the study to stop at any point

Withdrawal has no effect on your academic standing or relationship with the COMP2300 module.

## How will my data be stored?

- All observation data is stored locally on the project team's secure devices only.
- No data is uploaded to external services or cloud platforms.
- Your anonymous participant code (e.g., "P01") is the only identifier in observation sheets and reports.
- Consent forms are stored separately from observation data.
- All data is retained only for the duration of the COMP2300 module assessment period, then securely deleted.

## How will results be used?

Results will appear in our COMP2300 D7 Evaluation Report as aggregated and de-identified findings. No individual participant will be identifiable from the report.
