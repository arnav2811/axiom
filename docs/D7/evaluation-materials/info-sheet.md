# Participant Information Sheet — Footprint Lens Usability Evaluation

**Study Title:** Usability Evaluation of Footprint Lens: Contextual Choice Coach
**Researchers:** Arnav Mishra and Keshav Singhal (COMP2300 Group)
**Module:** COMP2300 Software Engineering Group Project

---

## What is this study about?

We are conducting a usability evaluation of **Footprint Lens**, a desktop application that helps track household carbon emissions using India-specific data. We would like you to try using the application and share your thoughts about the experience.

## What will I be asked to do?

- Complete **5 short tasks** using the Footprint Lens application (e.g., recording an activity, viewing a chart, exporting data)
- **Think aloud** while completing the tasks — tell us what you're thinking as you use the software
- Fill out a **short questionnaire** about your experience (System Usability Scale + a few custom questions)
- Participate in a brief **debrief conversation** (5 minutes)

The session will take approximately **45–60 minutes** in total.

## Will it be recorded?

Yes — with your permission, we will make a **screen recording** and an **audio recording** of the session. These recordings will be used only for analysis by the research team and will not be shared publicly.

## What data will be collected?

- Task completion times and success/failure
- Your spoken thoughts during tasks (think-aloud)
- Your questionnaire responses
- Your debrief comments

**We will NOT collect any sensitive personal data** (e.g., health data, political beliefs, religious views, sexual orientation) as defined by the UK GDPR.

## Is participation voluntary?

**Yes, completely.** You can:
- Choose not to participate
- Skip any task or question
- Stop the session at any time without giving a reason
- Withdraw your data after the session (contact us within 7 days)

There is no penalty for withdrawing.

## How will my data be used?

- Your data will be **anonymised** (identified by participant number, e.g., P1, P2)
- Results will be reported in our COMP2300 D7 evaluation report
- Recordings will be **deleted** after analysis (within 30 days)
- No data will be shared outside the COMP2300 module assessment
