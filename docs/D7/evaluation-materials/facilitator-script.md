# Facilitator Script — Footprint Lens Usability Evaluation

**Version:** Final (Updated for v1.0.0)
**Facilitator:** Arnav Mishra

---

## Before the Session

- [ ] Application running on evaluation laptop with 3 pre-loaded sample records
- [ ] Screen recording software (OBS) ready
- [ ] Audio recorder tested
- [ ] Printed materials: information sheet, consent form, task cards (5), questionnaire, observation sheet
- [ ] Stopwatch/timer ready
- [ ] Quiet room with minimal distractions

---

## 1. Welcome (2 minutes)

> "Hello, and thank you for taking the time to participate in our evaluation today. My name is Arnav, and this is Keshav who will be observing and taking notes."
>
> "We're evaluating a desktop application called Footprint Lens, which helps track household carbon emissions with India-specific data. We'd like you to try using it and tell us what you think."
>
> "I want to emphasise that **we're testing the software, not you**. There are no right or wrong answers. If something is confusing or difficult, that's the software's problem, not yours. Your honest feedback will help us improve it."
>
> "The session will take about 45–60 minutes. You can stop at any time."

---

## 2. Information Sheet and Consent (3 minutes)

> "Before we begin, please read this information sheet which explains what we're doing and how your data will be used."

[Hand participant the information sheet]

> "Do you have any questions about the study?"

[Answer questions]

> "If you're happy to continue, please read and sign the consent form."

[Hand consent form; wait for signature; countersign]

---

## 3. Think-Aloud Practice (5 minutes)

> "During this session, I'd like you to **think aloud** — that means saying everything that comes to mind as you work. Tell me what you're looking at, what you're thinking, what you're trying to do, and what you expect to happen."
>
> "Let's practice first with something unrelated. Can you tell me how many windows are in your house or flat? Think aloud as you count."

[Let participant practice thinking aloud for 1–2 minutes]

> "Great, that's exactly what I mean. Try to keep that up during the tasks. If you go quiet, I might gently remind you to keep talking, but don't worry if you forget sometimes."

---

## 4. Tasks (25–30 minutes)

> "Now I'm going to give you a series of tasks to complete with the application. I'll hand you each task on a card. Please read it aloud, then try to complete it while thinking aloud."
>
> "I won't be able to help you during the tasks — I need to see how the software works without guidance. If you get completely stuck, just say so and we'll move on."

### Task 1: Record Electricity Usage

[Hand Task Card 1]

> "Please read the card and begin when you're ready."

[Start timer when participant begins. Record on observation sheet: success/fail, time, errors, notes]

[After completion or max time:]
> "Thank you. On the back of the card, please rate how easy or difficult that task was on the 1–7 scale."

[Repeat for Tasks 2–5]

### Facilitator Reminders (use sparingly)

If participant goes silent for 15+ seconds:
> "What are you thinking about right now?"
> "What are you looking for?"

If participant asks for help:
> "What would you try if I wasn't here?"
> "Take your best guess."

If participant is clearly stuck (3+ minutes with no progress):
> "Let's move on to the next task. That's perfectly fine — it tells us something useful about the design."

---

## 5. Post-Task Questionnaire (10 minutes)

> "Thank you for completing the tasks. Now I'd like you to fill out a short questionnaire about your overall experience with the application."

[Hand SUS questionnaire + custom questions]

> "Please answer based on your experience today. There are no right or wrong answers."

[Wait for completion]

---

## 6. Debrief (5 minutes)

> "Thank you. I have a few final questions:"

1. "What was your **overall impression** of Footprint Lens?"
2. "Was there anything that **surprised** you about the application?"
3. "Was there anything you found **particularly difficult** or **confusing**?"
4. "Is there anything you think the application should do that it **doesn't currently do**?"
5. "Any other comments or suggestions?"

[Record responses]

> "Thank you very much for your time and feedback. It's been really helpful. Do you have any final questions for us?"

[End session]
