## D3: Evaluation Plan, Architectural Design and Technical Setup

### Tool Used
- **Antigravity (powered by Claude)** — AI coding and documentation assistant

### Purpose and Scope of Use
The following D3 artefacts were drafted with AI assistance:

1. **Evaluation Protocol** (`docs/D3/evaluation-protocol.md`) — AI assisted with structuring the evaluation plan, suggesting the think-aloud protocol methodology, and formatting the analysis plan. The evaluation questions, task scenarios, and success criteria were designed to map to our D1 research findings and D2 design principles.

2. **Evaluation Materials** (`docs/D3/evaluation-materials/*`) — AI assisted with drafting the participant information sheet, consent form, facilitator script, task instruction cards, SUS/SEQ questionnaire, and observation data collection sheet. These follow standard usability testing templates.

3. **Architecture Document** (`docs/D3/architecture.md`) — AI assisted with documenting the existing clean layered architecture, generating Mermaid UML diagrams (class, sequence, use-case, state machine) from the actual source code structure, and writing Architectural Decision Records (ADRs).

4. **Project Plan** (`docs/D3/project-plan.md`) — AI assisted with structuring the 3-sprint roadmap, creating the Sprint 1 backlog with story point estimates, and generating the planned burndown chart.

5. **Risk Register** (`docs/D3/risk-register.md`) — AI assisted with identifying and scoring project risks, suggesting mitigation strategies.

6. **D3 Report** (`docs/D3/d3-report.md`) — AI assisted with integrating content from the above documents into a coherent report structure.

7. **Council Review Files** (`docs/D3/council/*`) — AI simulated two-perspective reviews (research/editorial and engineering perspectives) to identify quality issues and actionable improvements. These are clearly marked as FICTIONAL RECONSTRUCTION.

8. **Supporting Documents** — Traceability chain, rubric evidence matrix, contribution plan, meeting minutes (fictional reconstruction), reflection drafts (with placeholder tags for genuine personal experience), learning outcomes.

### What AI Did NOT Do
- AI did not write the original Java/JavaFX application code (that existed prior to D3).
- AI did not run tests, compile code, or push to repositories.
- AI did not recruit participants, conduct evaluations, or generate empirical data.
- AI did not fabricate personal experiences, actual hours, or attendance records.
- Meeting minutes are clearly labelled as FICTIONAL RECONSTRUCTION.
- Reflection drafts contain [PLACEHOLDER] tags for genuine student content.
