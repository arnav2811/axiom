# Individual Reflective Report --- D2

Building on D1's evidence-based design, I translated research findings
into a conceptual specification. I developed the initial HTML/CSS
prototype, collaborated on user stories and design concepts, and helped
consolidate the selected interaction model. Peer critique exposed that
my interactive prototype exceeded the intended LO-FI fidelity, prompting
me to rebuild it as a simpler Figma wireframe. This reinforced the
importance of matching prototype fidelity to design maturity and
validating information architecture before implementation.

Our process combined Design Thinking's empathise and ideate stages with
iterative prototyping and design critique. I substantially restructured
the report, connecting D1 findings and design principles to
MoSCoW-prioritised stories, acceptance criteria, P0--P6 interaction
states and verification. Aligning these artefacts with flowcharts,
meeting decisions and the Figma prototype strengthened requirements
traceability and gave the team a specification for D3. I also proposed
gamification, reward incentives and accessibility enhancements, subject
to feasibility and user validation.

Critically, my preference for early completion sometimes restricted
divergent exploration, while premature report polishing risked rework. I
should have sought design feedback earlier. Future practice will
prioritise requirements validation, earlier review checkpoints and
incremental QA to improve efficiency without sacrificing design quality.
